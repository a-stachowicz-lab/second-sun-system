GALVANIC MATRIX

We prevent failures before they occur.

The Principle

ΔV > 0.50 V → mandatory isolation or sacrificial anode

Direct contact between conductive materials with a high potential difference causes galvanic corrosion. The risk class depends on the environment. In dry conditions, higher ΔV is tolerated. In humid or saltwater environments, even small differences become critical.

Risk Classes

Special Rules

  • Stainless Steel — Strongly alloy-dependent (304, 316L). Special rule required.
  • Brass (Cu+Zn) — Dezinification possible in aggressive media.
  • CFRP (Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer) — Electrically conductive. No electrode potential. Critical with aluminium.
  • ITO (Indium Tin Oxide) — Transparent conductor. No standard E°.

Core Rule

Gold on copper? Always use a nickel barrier.

ΔV > 0.50 V? Isolate or use a sacrificial anode.
